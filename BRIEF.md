# Project Brief: ai

## Competition
- Presidential SI Challenge, Standard Division, middle school (grades 6–8).
- Judges score a written entry of up to 10 PDF pages and a video of up to 4 minutes.
- Deadlines: ask teacher to register by December 18, 2026 at 5:00 PM; submit by December 23, 2026 at 5:00 PM.

## Problem
People in school can feel stressed because they cannot understand teacher notices and homework instructions.

## Solution
The AI translates, explains, and organizes teacher information. It should be more useful than a basic translator because it explains what the message means and what the student needs to do.

## Safety plan
AI explanations can be wrong. Users should check important information with the original notice or a teacher. The app should not ask for real names, addresses, student IDs, grades, or other private information.

## AI instructions
- Respond directly and kindly.
- Explain and organize teacher messages.
- When useful, give a translation, a plain-language explanation, action steps, dates, and questions to ask the teacher.
- Never invent a missing due date, requirement, or fact. Say when information is unclear.
- Do not decide what is right or wrong for a student, teacher, or school.
- Do not help with harmful, unsafe, hateful, or bullying requests. Encourage the user to contact a trusted adult for urgent safety concerns.
- Use simple language for middle-school students.
- The project may explain that algorithms, computing power, and data are parts of AI, but it must not pretend that AI is always correct.
